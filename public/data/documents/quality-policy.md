## 品質政策 Quality Policy Statement

智騰半導體（WiseSemi）秉持以下品質政策，作為公司全體同仁執行日常工作的最高指導原則：

**符合法規、客戶優先、品質為本、持續改善**

| 政策要項 | 說明 |
|---|---|
| 符合法規 | 遵循國內外相關法令、產業標準與客戶合約要求，確保產品與服務合法合規。 |
| 客戶優先 | 以客戶需求為核心，主動傾聽並回應客戶期待，追求客戶滿意與長期信任。 |
| 品質為本 | 品質是公司經營的根本，全員參與品質管理系統，落實各項作業標準與紀律。 |
| 持續改善 | 透過數據分析、稽核與檢討機制，不斷優化流程、提升效率與產品可靠度。 |

---

WiseSemi is committed to the following Quality Policy as the guiding principle for everyone in the company:

**Compliance with Regulations · Customer First · Quality-Oriented · Continuous Improvement**

| Policy Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| Regulatory Compliance | Comply with applicable laws, industry standards, and customer contractual requirements. |
| Customer First | Put customer needs at the center of every decision, and pursue customer satisfaction and long-term trust. |
| Quality-Oriented | Quality is the foundation of our business; every employee participates in the quality management system and follows work standards with discipline. |
| Continuous Improvement | Continuously optimize processes and improve reliability through data analysis, audits, and management review. |

---

## 適用範圍 Scope

本品質政策適用於智騰半導體全體員工，並作為各部門制定品質目標的依據。

This Quality Policy applies to all WiseSemi employees and serves as the basis for setting departmental quality objectives.
